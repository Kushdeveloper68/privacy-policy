Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

KV Agency ("we," "our," or "us") operates automation tools connected to our Instagram account to help us respond to comments and messages from our audience. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our Instagram account (by commenting on our posts or Reels), our automation tool may access:

  • Your Instagram username
  • The text content of your comment
  • A unique comment/interaction identifier provided by Instagram's API
  • The post or Reel the comment was made on

We do not access your private profile information, follower list, browsing activity, or any data beyond what Instagram's official Graph API provides for public comment interactions.

2. How We Use This Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Identify when a comment contains a specific keyword (e.g. "WEBSITE" or "PRICE")
  • Send an automated private reply (direct message) relevant to that comment

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond responding to your comment.

3. Data Storage

Comment text and usernames are processed in real time to determine whether an automated reply should be sent. We do not maintain a permanent database of comment content beyond what is necessary to prevent duplicate replies to the same comment.

4. Third-Party Services

Our automation interacts exclusively with Meta's official Instagram Graph API. We do not use unofficial or third-party scraping tools. Meta's own data practices are governed by their Data Policy.

5. Your Rights

You may request that we delete any data associated with your Instagram username by contacting us using the details below. You can also control your own data directly through your Instagram privacy settings.

6. Data Deletion

To request deletion of any data we may hold related to your interactions with our account, email us at the address below with your Instagram username. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
